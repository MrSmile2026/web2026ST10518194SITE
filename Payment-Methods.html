Payment Methods

KO TECH supports a range of secure payment options so you can check out however suits you best. All transactions are encrypted end-to-end.

Credit / Debit Card

Visa, Mastercard and American Express accepted. Charged securely at checkout.

PayPal

Pay using your PayPal balance, linked card or bank account.

Payflex

Split your purchase into 4 interest-free instalments over 6 weeks.

EFT / Bank Transfer

Pay directly from your bank account using our EFT banking details.

Mobile Wallets

Apple Pay and Samsung Pay supported on compatible devices.

Cash On Delivery

Available on selected orders under R5 000 within major metro areas.

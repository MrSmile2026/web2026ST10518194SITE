KO TECH GALLERY + ORDER TRACKING UPDATE

Gallery.html
- Coral-orange editorial layout based on the supplied screenshot: Our Work title, black CTA and two continuously moving rows of product photos.
- Hover or focus pauses the movement and displays image titles; click an image to open the lightbox.
- Reduced-motion users can scroll the rows without automatic movement.

Track-Order.html
- Signed-in customers see their Supabase orders automatically.
- Click an order to see a five-step status timeline, saved update history, order details and PDF slip.
- Visitors without an account see a sign-in option. Browser demo orders can be tracked only on their original browser using the private access code.
- Press Refresh status to get the latest progress. Status moves forward only when the store updates the order in Supabase.

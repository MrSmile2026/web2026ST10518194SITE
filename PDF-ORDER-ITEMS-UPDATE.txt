KO TECH - CUSTOMER ORDER PDF UPDATE
===================================
The PDF slip downloadable after checkout and from Track Order / Account now shows:
- Product photo for every ordered item (or a placeholder if unavailable)
- Product name and product code
- Quantity, unit price, and line total
- Subtotal, discounts, delivery and order total
- Customer and delivery information, payment and order status
- Private tracking link

The browser generates the PDF locally with no paid PDF service. Images are taken
from your own site's Images folder and converted to JPEG for the PDF.
Real customer accounts and persistent orders still require Supabase setup as
described in SETUP-ACCOUNTS.md. The PDF is an order slip, not proof of payment.
